Frequently asked questions

Are listed hunting guides verified?

A directory record is not credential confirmation. Verify licensing and service scope with the state board.

Can I use a general season summary to plan a hunt?

No. Verify the current species, unit, residency, and year-specific regulations with ADF&G.

Is a taxidermy listing a guarantee of service?

No. Confirm current operation, licensing where required, species acceptance, cost, and timeline directly.
