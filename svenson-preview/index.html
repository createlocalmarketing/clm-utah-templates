Answers Before You Book

Fishing Charter FAQs

What kinds of fishing charters does Svenson offer in Ketchikan?

Choose from salmon, halibut, and combination charter options. Confirm current availability and trip details directly with Svenson.

Can I book a fishing charter during my Ketchikan cruise stop?

It depends on your ship schedule, trip availability, pickup plan, and return buffer. Give Svenson your ship and port times before booking.

How much does a Ketchikan fishing charter cost?

Confirm the current total, price basis, inclusions, deposit, and cancellation policy. The public rates and FAQ pages showed a price conflict during the audit, so the rebuild should use one verified rate source.

Do I need an Alaska fishing license?

Requirements depend on residency and age. Check current Alaska Department of Fish and Game rules for your situation. The license page should be reviewed and dated.

What should I bring on a Ketchikan fishing trip?

Ask Svenson for the current clothing, footwear, personal-item, and accessibility guidance for your trip date.

Can Svenson process and ship my catch?

Confirm available processing and shipping options, what is included, extra costs, timing, and limits before the charter.

What if weather changes or my cruise schedule shifts?

Review the current weather, delay, cancellation, and missed-ship policies with Svenson before booking. Make sure cruise timing is confirmed in writing.
