Frequently asked questions

How do I book a Svenson fishing charter?

Contact Svenson at 907-617-7465 or use its current booking route to confirm date, trip, group size, price, and terms.

Does this site list all Ketchikan businesses?

No. It is a sourced research directory and does not claim exhaustive coverage.

Can I use the Safety Guide during an emergency?

It is educational and offline reference material, not dispatch or live monitoring. Call 911 when possible and follow dispatcher instructions.
