Frequently asked questions

Who reviews Indigenous history pages?

They should be developed with appropriate Indigenous community-led sources and reviewers; no single reviewer can speak for all Alaska Native peoples.

Can I reuse a historic photo from an archive?

Check the repository’s rights, attribution, privacy, and reproduction terms for that exact item before publication.

How are corrections handled?

Each chapter should show sources and a correction contact so specific factual errors can be reviewed and documented.
