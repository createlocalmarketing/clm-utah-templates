This is the explicit inventory for every homepage content card and each carousel banner. It is a page-level build checklist, not a substitute for the full sitemap/page-family inventory tracked in issue #10.

## Non-negotiable card acceptance checklist

Every user-facing content box must include:
- A specific, relevant, licensed image in the WordPress Media Library.
- SEO-aware image filename, media title, accurate alt text, and image title.
- Visible title and a distinct subtitle.
- A concise, useful description.
- A clear button with a working destination.
- Internal links and page/query mapping recorded in the site blueprint.

Image direction: real, editorial-quality Envato photography or approved project/brand imagery. Show a balanced mix of ethnicities, genders, and age groups across the site. Do not use client/lead headshots as agency imagery. Do not use drawn/animated, AI-painted, or generic placeholder art. Brand logos and icons may accompany text, but cannot replace a card image.

Status key: **INCOMPLETE** means the current live page does not meet this card standard yet. **VERIFY** means a human must confirm the exact image and destination before it can be marked complete.

## Completion gate

Do not mark a card complete until its image is present in the Media Library, metadata and copy are populated, the button resolves, the section renders consistently at desktop/mobile widths, and its target page is linked in the master SEO map.
