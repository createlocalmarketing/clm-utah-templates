Frequently asked questions

Are charter rates and availability guaranteed here?

No. Confirm current rates, available dates, inclusions, and policy terms directly with the operator.

Do I need an Alaska fishing license?

Requirements depend on age, residency, species, and current rules. Check Alaska Fish and Game before your trip.

Will my ship departure be guaranteed?

No charter site can guarantee a ship return. Confirm logistics and allow a conservative buffer with the operator and cruise line.
