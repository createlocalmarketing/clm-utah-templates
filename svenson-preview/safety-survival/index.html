Frequently asked questions

Is this a CPR certification course?

No. It is a learning aid and does not provide certification or replace instructor-led practice.

Does the offline app call rescuers?

No. It cannot send an SOS, dispatch help, or monitor your location.

What should I do in a real emergency?

Call 911 when available and follow dispatcher instructions; use recognized training and seek professional care.
