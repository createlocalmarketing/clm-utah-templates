Frequently asked questions

Are deals available right now?

Only an offer with current provider approval and visible dates and terms should be shown as active. This review directory does not invent or imply an unverified promotion.

What must an offer disclose?

The provider, exact customer benefit, eligibility, exclusions, valid dates or capacity, redemption instructions, booking route, and sponsorship disclosure.

What if the offer has expired?

It should be removed from active results or clearly archived with its expired status.
